Here's the full three-level taxonomy:

1. Anchors & Connectors

Anchors & Fasteners → Concrete/Masonry Anchors, Toggle Bolts & Hollow-Wall Anchors, Threaded Rod & Studs, Other Anchors & Fasteners
Structural Connectors → Beam Clamps & Pipe Clamps, Cable Tray Connectors, Strut/Unistrut Connectors, Other Structural Connectors
2. Boxes & Enclosures

By Type → Device/Switch Boxes, Junction/Pull Boxes, Weatherproof Boxes, Floor Boxes, Fan-Rated Boxes, Other Boxes & Enclosures
Covers & Plates → Blank Covers, Wall Plates & Device Plates, Weatherproof In-Use Covers, Box Covers & Extension Rings, Other Covers & Plates
NEMA Enclosures → NEMA 1 (Indoor), NEMA 3R (Rainproof), NEMA 4/4X (Watertight), Other NEMA Enclosures
3. Builders Products

Strut & Framing → Strut Channel, Strut Fittings & Nuts, Other Strut & Framing
Cable Management → Cable Ties & Zip Ties, Cable Staples & Clips, Cable Duct & Wiring Duct, Other Cable Management
Supports & Hangers → Hanger Rods & All-Thread, Beam/Trapeze Hangers, Pipe Hangers & Clamps, Other Supports & Hangers
4. Conduit & Raceway

By Material → EMT, PVC, RMC/Rigid Metal, IMC, FMC/Flexible Metal, ENT/Smurf Tube, Other Conduit

Wireways & Cable Tray → Metal Wireways/Panduit Duct, Ladder Cable Tray, Solid Bottom Cable Tray, Other Wireways & Cable Tray
5. Motors, Controls & Sensors

Motor Controls → Starters/Contactors/VFDs
Relays & Timers → Control Relays, Timer Switches & Astronomic Timers, Other Relays & Timers
Sensors → Photocells/Light Sensors, Temperature Sensors & Thermostats, Current Sensors & CT Clamps, Other Sensors
Pilot Devices → Push Buttons, Pilot Lights & Indicators, Selector Switches, Other Pilot Devices
6. Breakers

By Type → Standard Circuit Breakers, GFCI Breakers, AFCI Breakers
7. Panels & Distribution

By Type → Load Centers/Panelboards, Meter Sockets/Meter Mains, Other Panels
Disconnects & Safety Switches → Fusible Safety Switches, Non-Fusible Safety Switches, Other Disconnects
Surge Protection → Whole-House/Panel SPDs, Point-of-Use Surge Protectors, Other Surge Protection
Transformers → Control/Buck-Boost Transformers, Other Transformers
Fuses → Cartridge Fuses, Glass/Automotive Fuses, Other Fuses
8. Fittings

Grounding Fittings → Ground Rods & Clamps, Grounding Connectors & Clamps, Other Grounding Fittings
Liquidtight Fittings → Liquidtight Connectors, Other Liquidtight Fittings
Reducers & Adapters → Conduit Reducers & Adapters, Nipples & Extensions, Other Reducers & Adapters
9. HVAC

HVAC Controls → Thermostats, HVAC Contactors, Other HVAC Controls
HVAC Motors & Components → HVAC Fan & Blower Motors, Capacitors (Run/Start), Other HVAC Components
HVAC Disconnects & Protection → AC Disconnect Switches, Fuse Holders & Blocks, Other HVAC Protection
10. Lighting

By Type → LED Bulbs, Fluorescent, Fixtures, Recessed/Cans, Other Lighting
Lighting Controls → Daylight/Photocell Controls, Motion & Occupancy Controls, Dimmer Controls, Other Lighting Controls
Emergency & Exit Lighting → Exit Signs, Emergency Lighting Units, Other Emergency Lighting
11. Line Construction Material

Overhead Wire & Cable → Triplex/Quadruplex URD, AAC/ACSR Overhead Conductors, Other Overhead Wire
Pole Hardware & Insulators → Insulators, Deadend & Strain Clamps, Other Pole Hardware
Underground Distribution → URD Cable, URD Splices & Terminations, Other Underground Distribution
12. Telecom

Data & Telecom Cable → Cat 5e, Cat 6/6A, Fiber Optic, Coaxial, Other Telecom Cable
Data Connectors & Jacks → RJ45 Jacks & Keystone Modules, Coax Connectors & Splitters, Other Data Jacks & Connectors
Telecom Enclosures & Patch Panels → Patch Panels, Telecom Enclosures & Brackets, Other Telecom Enclosures
13. Tools & Testers

Hand Tools → Pliers & Cutters, Screwdrivers & Nut Drivers, Fish Tape & Pull Line, Conduit Benders, Other Hand Tools
Test & Measurement → Multimeters, Clamp Meters, Circuit Testers & Voltage Detectors, Continuity & Wire Testers, Other Test Equipment
Power Tool Accessories → Drill Bits & Hole Saws, Saw Blades & Cutting Accessories, Other Power Tool Accessories
14. Wire & Cable

By Type → THHN/THWN Building Wire, Romex/NM-B Cable, MC/Armored Cable, UF Underground Feeder, SER/Service Entrance, Other Wire & Cable
Specialty Wire & Cable → Control Cable, Tray Cable (TC), Welding Cable, Low-Voltage/Thermostat Wire, Other Specialty Cable
15. Receptacles

By Type → Duplex Receptacles, GFCI Receptacles, USB Receptacles, Twist-Lock Receptacles, Range/Dryer Receptacles, Other Receptacles
16. Switches & Dimmers

By Type → Toggle Switches, 3-Way Switches, 4-Way Switches, Dimmers, Occupancy Sensors, Other Switches
17. Connectors & Terminations

By Type → Wire Nuts, Lugs, Terminal Blocks, Other Connectors
That's 17 main categories, 44 subcategories, and 131 item types in total. There's also an Uncategorized catch-all (Needs Review → Unclassified Items) for parts the classifier can't place.
